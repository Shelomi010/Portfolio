# Shelomi Suzan Nebu — Personal Portfolio

A personal, editorial portfolio for **Shelomi Suzan Nebu**, a first-semester BBA student in Digital Marketing and E-commerce at **Asian School of Business**, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala.

Art direction inspired by **Maryl Gonzalez** (*"Curiosity in Orbit"*), with structural clarity and disciplined layout drawn from **Russell Numo** and **Valentin Gassend**.

---

## ✦ Design & Art Direction

- **Art Direction**: *"Curiosity in Orbit"* — deep midnight aubergine opening atmosphere (`#161224`), flowing into warm editorial ivory paper (`#faf7f2`) with deep plum typography and soft lavender/gold celestial accents.
- **Truthful Identity**: Grounded in Shelomi's real student journey, natural curiosity, and design interests. No invented metrics, no fake agencies, and no artificial consultant claims.
- **Visual Centerpiece**: Dedicated showcase of **Umami House — House of Flavours**, a five-piece visual branding and promotional coursework project created in Canva.
- **Signature Interaction**: An elegant **orbiting star (`✦`)** that tracks smoothly with scroll progress along the page margin, accompanied by an accessible **"Pause motion"** toggle.
- **Cinematic Artwork Lightbox**: A built-in modal viewer allowing visitors to inspect each design full-size with preserved portrait proportions and design notes.
- **Direct & Honest Contact**: A personal closing thought and direct email touchpoint (`nebushelomi@gmail.com`) with 1-click clipboard copy.

## 🚀 How to Preview Locally

No build tools, bundlers, or servers required:
1. Open the folder `d:\portfolio thingy\portfolio` in File Explorer.
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to view the website in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
3. Try clicking on any Umami House poster to open the full-scale image viewer.
4. Try clicking the **"Pause motion"** pill in the top header to see the accessibility motion toggle in action.

---

## 🌐 Deploying to GitHub Pages (Free)

1. Create a new public repository on [github.com](https://github.com) (e.g. `shelomi-portfolio`).
2. Upload the project files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, `README.md`, and the `assets/` folder).
3. Go to **Repository Settings** &rarr; **Pages**.
4. Set **Source** to `Deploy from a branch`, select `main` (or `master`) and `/ (root)` folder, then click **Save**.
5. Within 1 minute, the live site will be available at:  
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/shelomi-portfolio`
